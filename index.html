Current focus

Corn Cobs

Corn cobs are collected through collectors, plantations and logistics partners. Through processing partners they are milled and sieved into granules and powder. Formats and particle sizes are discussed according to each buyer's requirements.

CollectCollectors, plantations, logistics partners
Mill & sieveVia processing partners
Granules & powderFormats and sizes per buyer requirements

Applications

Where processed corn cobs may fit

Suitability depends on processing, specification and the buyer's own testing.

Cosmetic & personal care

Appropriately processed corn cob powder can serve as a physical exfoliating material in selected formulations.

Industrial

Corn cob granules and powder can be used as absorbent or polishing media.

Specifications, particle sizes and suitability for a given use are discussed per buyer requirement and confirmed through the buyer's own testing.
